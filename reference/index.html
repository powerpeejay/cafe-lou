Our Story

A Place You'll
Come Back To

Café Lou started as a simple idea: a neighbourhood spot where people could slow down, eat something good, and feel at home. No fuss — just great ingredients, honest cooking, and a warm welcome.

We pour single-origin coffee, bake our pastries fresh each morning, and change the lunch menu with the seasons. Come once and you'll know the feeling.
